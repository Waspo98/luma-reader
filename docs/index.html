Luma Reader

Fluid, expressive, and distraction-free e-book reading

Luma Reader is a modern e-book reading application built on Kotlin Multiplatform, Jetpack Compose, and the Readium 3.0 mobile reading engine. Designed with Material Design 3 Expressive aesthetics, organic physics-based spring animations, and private cloud synchronization.

Private Cloud Sync

Synchronize reading progress, bookmarks, shelves, and catalog across devices via Google Drive appDataFolder.

Readium 3.0 Engine

High-fidelity EPUB pagination, custom typography, hyphenation, and immersive themes.

Local First

No account walls or external tracking. Your library and notes remain 100% yours.
